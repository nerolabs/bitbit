# M0 — the systemic spec: holding the trilemma by composition

**Status: canonical M0 spec.** This is the single source of truth for *what M0
claims*, *how it is held*, and *what a red team or builder should attack*. It
supersedes the finding-by-finding design notes written during the internal
hardening pass (now under [`/archive/design-history/`](../../archive/) — kept for
history, not for planning). Read it with [`../TENETS.md`](../TENETS.md) Part 0
(the canon) and [`m0-sybil-rebind.md`](m0-sybil-rebind.md) (the bond as-built).

**Provenance.** The reframe here is the builder-side adoption of the research
capstone `09-m0-as-composition.md` (in the read-only review archive
`silt-reviews/research/research-outcome/`, with supporting memos 01–08). The
as-built mechanisms are the internal hardening pass H1–H6. This doc fuses the two:
the *claim* (memo 09) and the *shipped surfaces* (the hardening pass).

## 0. The reset, in one paragraph

For months the loop was: design a bond/puzzle/proof → hand it to the red team →
it breaks → try a variation. That loop is **infinite by construction**, and the
red team was right every time — because the claim under test, *"this primitive is
Sybil-proof,"* is **false by theorem** (Douceur: under free identity minting and
no permanent center, no single primitive can prevent Sybils). You cannot
out-engineer an impossibility result. The escape is not a better primitive; it is
a **different claim**. M0's Sybil corner is a **systemic property held by the
composition of many parts in tension**, not a lone Sybil-proof mechanism. This
doc states that systemic claim precisely, shows the interlock that makes it true,
maps it to what is shipped, and redefines what a passing red team looks like.

---

## 1. Why the primitive hunt was unwinnable

Douceur's result, the anonymity trilemma (memo 01), the MLE theorem (memo 02),
and the recurring "no-center tax" are the same shape: **walls that stand for any
*isolated* mechanism.** When the builder said "this bond makes standing
Sybil-proof," the statement was false *by theorem*, and the red team breaking it
was a correct proof of a known fact — not new information. Retiring
*"is this primitive Sybil-proof?"* as the unit of test is the whole reset.

## 2. The move: make forging-standing *identical to* honest provision

Douceur leaves exactly one door open: you cannot *prevent* or *detect* cheap
Sybils, but you can make a **successful** Sybil economically indistinguishable
from an honest contributor. If earning N units of standing requires the genuine
work of N honest providers, then a "Sybil farm" that actually earns N standings
*is* N honest providers' worth of real resource — and it is not an attack, it is
the network working. This converts *"Sybil-resistance"* (impossible: detect
fakes) into **standing-concentration measurement** (hard but approachable: is
*real* standing too concentrated in one operator?). The threat does not vanish —
it moves from cryptography, where it is impossible, to cryptoeconomics +
measurement, where it is merely hard.

## 3. The systemic claim — what M0 actually asserts

> **Systemic Sybil-soundness.** Under free minting and no permanent center, for
> any adversary A:
> **(C1 — no discount)** the cost for A to acquire a fraction *q* of total
> consensus standing is at least `(1 − o(1)) · q · C_honest`, where `C_honest` is
> the real resource an honest provider pays for that much *served, sustained*
> standing; **and (C2 — no quiet capture)** the objective concentration metric
> keeps the minimum colluding **operator** set that reaches the quorum-capture
> threshold above a target *k*, measured Byzantine-robustly.
>
> Equivalently: **no strategy earns consensus-controlling standing more cheaply
> than honestly providing that much real, served, sustained, address-diverse
> storage — and honest wealth cannot silently concentrate past capture.**

C1 is the fold (forging ≡ provision). C2 is the residue C1 cannot touch (an
honest whale). Both must hold. `C_honest` is a **product of orthogonal,
non-substitutable factors** — you cannot trade surplus disk for missing address
diversity, or buy your way past elapsed time:

```
C_honest  ∝   D   ×   A   ×   T   ×   B
              │       │       │       │
   distinct sealed    address/AS      elapsed time +      served bandwidth to
   disk per identity  diversity       witnessed audit     real, attested demand
   (bond + unique     (keyspace       history             (anti-wash)
    replica)           placement)
```

Non-substitutability is what turns *defense in depth* (additive; peel one layer
at a time) into **entangled defense** (multiplicative; every cheap shortcut on
one axis trips a different axis's check).

> **⚠️ Target composition vs. shipped subset — state which.** `C_honest ∝ D×A×T×B`
> is the *target*. **Today, consensus standing is gated by the D (bond) axis
> alone.** The **B axis (served demand)** is an unbuilt track (D-DEMAND / #181);
> **A (address diversity)** is enforced at the DHT layer (H5) but does **not** yet
> enter the standing number; **T (time)** ships for standing *retention only*
> (decay/TTL forces continuous re-proof) — there is **no acquisition-time
> accrual**, so standing is acquired the instant D bytes are proven and
> acquisition is priced by **D alone** (a time-of-acquisition ramp is deferred: a
> bare age gate is pre-farmable, and the only sound form is a continuous
> bond-anchored VDF, M1+). So the multiplicative
> "every shortcut trips another axis" property is **designed, not fully wired** —
> in force today it is closer to `C_honest ≈ D`. **C1 is therefore a *conditional*
> claim** (a theorem under the B5 hypotheses — §7, §10 — not an operative guarantee
> until B and A gate standing). Do not read §4's interlock table as fully live: it
> is the map of parts, with per-axis status in §6.

## 4. The interlock — how each part closes the previous part's hole

Sybil attacks are *reuse* attacks. Read each row as "the cheap shortcut → the
part that denies it → the axis it protects." The **elegance is in the conflicts:
the cheap way to do one thing is the expensive way to do another.**

| The economy of scale a Sybil wants | Denied by | Axis | Memo |
|---|---|---|---|
| One physical plot backs many identities | Size-bound public seed + labeling check → each identity needs its own plot | D | G2 bond |
| A plot of synthetic junk stands in for real storage | Standing tied to **unique sealed replicas of real network content** | D | 03 |
| Self-deal: publish your own junk, "store" it to yourself | Standing tracks **witnessed demand** (content others fetch), not mere possession | B | 01 |
| Attributing demand re-links who-fetched-what (breaks privacy) | **Reuse the blind-token primitive as an unlinkable demand receipt** | B ∩ privacy | 01 |
| Mint many keys near one target key, cheaply | **IP/AS-diversity buckets + eclipse hardening** — free keys buy nothing without address diversity | A | 08 |
| Cherry-pick cheap/lucrative content | **Content-blindness + hash placement** — content is assigned, not chosen | D | 02 |
| Prove a bond once, release the plot, and coast on old standing | **Retention decay — standing lapses without continuous re-proof (DecayStale + BondTTL)**. *Acquisition-time is NOT gated: instant-mint is priced by D alone; a time-of-acquisition ramp is deferred (§3, §6).* | T | 05 |
| An honest whale quietly concentrates real standing | **Shed/concentration metric: cost-to-corrupt / Nakamoto-over-operators**, gating the anchor training-wheels | C2 | 05 |

The single most under-exploited interlock is the **blind-token demand receipt**
(row 4): the *same* Chaumian primitive that provides publisher privacy resolves
the one place two M0 corners genuinely conflict (Sybil/accountability wants demand
attributed; privacy wants it unlinkable). Two corners that appear to fight are
reconciled by one part. Worth prototyping first — it is where "held in tension"
stops being a slogan and becomes a mechanism.

## 5. One ledger, read from two sides

The file-sharing plane and the trust plane are **not rival teams to reconcile.**
Done right they *would* be one ledger: the bytes you are paid to keep *are* the
stake that gives you a vote; the durability budget (S7) *is* the Sybil budget; the
audit that proves you still hold real content *is* the challenge that sustains your
standing. Splitting them created the tension; fusing them is the *design goal*.

> **⚠️ The fusion is NOT shipped, and deliberately so.** Today consensus standing
> comes **only** from a *dedicated, identity-keyed bond plot* (throwaway labels),
> **separate** from the useful shared content a node serves; served content and PoR
> audits mint **no** standing (§6 S2). The reason is the **γ→1/N shared-content
> shortcut** (§10 / #182): if standing were granted for holding a *shared*
> erasure-coded shard, one physical copy would answer for N pledges. Fusing served
> content into standing without that discount needs identity-keyed PoRep sealing,
> which does not yet exist — so **"C1 holds" is gated on this separation holding.**
> S7 and M0 are *designed* to be one problem with one answer; today they are two
> separate ledgers, and treating them as fused would re-open the exact Sybil break
> the separation prevents.

§4** — they are not the guarantee (the guarantee is the composition, §3), they are
the parts that make each axis's check real. **Verdict** = held / residual as of
this writing; all await the *external* re-verification against the systemic claim.

**Read the wall of "held" honestly:** S1–S8 are the *shipped* axis-checks, and even
they await external re-verification (they are "held" internally, not certified). The
**served-demand (B) axis is not shipped at all**, so today standing ≈ the bond (D)
axis alone; the full multiplicative interlock (§3) is the target, not the operative
state. The single core open problem that would let served content *safely* back
standing — the γ→1/N shared-content sealing boundary — is §10 / #182.

### Build-time guardrails (how we keep the composition honest)

Two invariants, enforced by tests, so a new mechanism cannot silently regress the
composition:

- **Invariant A — no standing without a verified, identity-bound,
  unpredictable-challenge, deduped, bond-gated proof.** Enforced by
  `core/credit/invariant_a_test.go`: a reflection guard forces *every* `*Ledger`
  method to be classified `mints`/`reduces`/`neutral`, and a behavioral guard
  proves no non-`mints` press lifts a bondless identity. A new standing press
  fails the build until classified.
- **Invariant B — a mechanism is not "shipped" until its safe configuration is
  the DEFAULT for the untrusted posture.** Enforced by
  `cmd/silt/invariant_b_test.go`: the default untrusted-validator config must deny
  the attack, per mechanism. Off-by-default (which bit us 3×) now fails loudly.

## 7. What "done" means now — the composition-level target (the seams)

Composition is not free, and red teams break composed systems **at the seams.**
These — not "break this primitive" — are the real attack surface, and what the
red team and the build team should now attack. Each is a target with a pass
condition. **Note the shape:** C1 (no discount) is close to achievable by
composition; **C2, cold-start, and the real-vs-wash-demand ratio are where M0 is
genuinely *held in tension* and may never be *closed*.** That is not a failure —
M0 asks to *hold* the trilemma, not resolve it — but the spec must say *which*
corners are closed and which are held.

1. **Re-pricing, not prevention (the wealth residue).** C1 does nothing against an
   actor who *honestly* provides ~40% of real storage and captures the quorum with
   no cheating. *Target:* does C2's shed/concentration metric bound the minimum
   colluding operator set below capture, under adversarially-skewed measurement?
   *Pass:* cost-to-corrupt over bond-distinct **operators**, Byzantine-robustly
   sampled, stays above *k*. **Most likely to be where M0 is *held*, not closed.**
   *Research (`B1-*`):* achievable bound is `k* ≥ k̂/M`, so **shed only when k̂ ≥
   k·M.** Sharpest engineering find — **compute the weight numerator from the
   committed on-chain bond ledger, not gossip** (the objective path already does),
   which kills the gossip-skew half of the skew+split attack outright; what remains
   skewable is only the off-chain size/clustering signals (Brahms + CPR harden
   those, down to a residual bias, not zero). **One measurement — Nakamoto-over-
   operators from the committed ledger — feeds three seams at once:** this shed, the
   private-lookup committee certification (D-PRIV build-track), and C2. Compute
   once, consume in three places.
2. **Cold-start / scaffolding-capture window.** "Standing tracks witnessed demand
   from reputable peers" bootstraps from itself; a young network has neither.
   *Target:* can an adversary capture the anchor-scaffolded young regime before it
   matures and sheds? *Pass:* maturity is reached before any feasible capture, and
   the anchors are plural + threshold so none is load-bearing (immutable #3).
3. **Self-dealing dominated only by real demand.** C1's anti-wash rests on *real*
   demand outweighing *fabricated* demand. *Target:* the ratio of attested-real to
   wash demand an adversary can manufacture. *Pass:* an economic argument (not a
   theorem) that honest demand dominates at realistic parameters — stated as the
   empirical condition it is.
4. **Privacy ↔ attribution linkage leak.** If the blind demand-receipt is
   imperfect, either privacy leaks or demand is forgeable. *Target:* can a
   colluding validator subset de-anonymize a fetcher *or* mint receipts without
   served bytes? *Status (held-in-tension):* D3 issuance-mixing severs the
   identity/IP/payment link (verified over real TCP). Two residual leaks remain —
   a demand receipt is forgeable with **zero** object bytes (the per-object PoR
   seed is public), and a bonded-mode receipt links fetch→standing key to a single
   validator — but both are **neutralized today by the firewall** (served/witnessed
   demand has *no* consensus consumer; §7 B-axis). They are **not closed**, and
   **must be** before any demand→standing fusion, or they re-open exactly the
   γ→1/N hole the firewall exists to hold.
5. **Operator clustering is heuristic.** Address-diversity counts *address
   groups*; "5 operators" vs "1 operator, 5 /24s" is a heuristic call. *Target:* an
   adversary with real cloud/AS diversity that evades operator-clustering. *Pass:*
   clustering by infra/timing/locality raises cost meaningfully — accepting that
   perfect operator identification is impossible without a trusted authority.
6. **Time-axis gaming (on-off / camouflage).** Standing-as-time-integral is
   beatable by banking reputation then defecting intermittently. *Target:* an
   adversary tuned to the audit/decay window. *Pass:* rolling-window + hysteretic
   accrual raises cost; no proven-optimal window exists, so this is bounded, not
   closed.
7. **New liveness/griefing surfaces.** Composed rules create new ways to stall
   (withholding demand receipts, refusing to attest). *Target:* can a minority
   grief liveness under the fused rules? *Pass:* liveness degrades gracefully, not
   catastrophically.

## 8. The builder ↔ red-team protocol

- **Stop testing per-primitive claims.** "Is this bond Sybil-proof?" is false by
  theorem; retire it as the unit of test.
- **Test the systemic claim (§3).** The red team's job: *find a strategy that earns
  consensus-controlling standing for less than `q · C_honest`, or that concentrates
  past capture, or that breaks a §7 seam.* A pass is: no such strategy at the
  network's declared parameters.
- **Stage the attack along the axes:** (i) per-axis reuse attacks (one disk → many
  ids; one IP → many nodes; instant standing; self-dealt content) — each denied by
  its part; (ii) cross-axis **seam** attacks (§7) — the real prize; (iii)
  concentration/shed attacks against C2; (iv) cold-start capture against the young
  regime.
- **Expect "held," not "closed," on some seams — and the spec says so.** A seam
  *held in tension* (bounded cost, documented residual) is a *pass* under M0's own
  definition; a seam silently assumed closed is the failure mode.

If the builder and red team both switch to this claim, the loop terminates: either
a seam falls (real, localized, fixable information) or the system holds at stated
parameters (M0 held). Either outcome is progress; the old loop produced neither.

## 9. Decisions (owner: Andrew)

The directions below are **decided** — derived from the accepted research package.
The full ledger, including the non-M0 decisions (D-DISCLOSURE, D-CRYPTO-AGILITY,
D-ANCHORS), lives in [`../decisions.md`](../decisions.md).

- **D-PRIV — ✅ DECIDED.** Access privacy is a *stated, layered tradeoff*, not a
  blob-layer absolute (memo 01: the anonymity trilemma is a hard wall). The
  *refusal to surveil* is absolute; access-unobservability is a metadata-layer
  goal held in tension (mixnet + private DHT + unlinkable tokens), bounded by the
  trilemma and anonymity-set size. Immutable #4 amended accordingly. **Also ship:**
  the D3 issuance-mixing residual (publisher IP+timing). Full H8 metadata stack is
  a later build track.
- **D-S7 — ✅ DIRECTION DECIDED + BUILT (H7/#95).** Relax "no token" to "no
  *speculative external* token": fund durability from an internal, escrowable,
  time-shiftable credit reserve (per-object escrow + serve auto-skim + rarest-shard
  bounty) — the *same ledger* as the Sybil budget (§5). Standing stays coin-free.
  **Center-less proof-of-*correct*-repair is built and adversarially verified** —
  correctness by **Merkle recompute** (reconstruct from k survivors, check the
  content address), retrievability by an **identity-bound Shacham–Waters PoR**, and a
  **care-link quorum** for center-less checking; the bounty releases iff both legs
  verify and an attributable correctness lie is bond-slashed. **The blind
  polynomial-commitment correctness leg the design preferred (transparent FRI-Binius)
  was pressure-tested and deferred**: no ring homomorphism GF(2⁸)→F_r means a
  prime-field commitment can't carry the RS relation, and no mature pure-Go char-2
  commitment exists to adopt (B8), so M0 ships the recompute floor — sound and
  content-blind, but **not bandwidth-blind** (an explicit non-goal). The durability
  *contract* ships **finite-but-renewable, not "perpetual"** — perpetual solvency
  needs `g > 0` (credit-denominated cost decline), which 2020s hardware no longer
  guarantees; **instrument `g` is built** (`credit.G`/`Horizon`/`CostPerRepair`), so
  silt *earns* "perpetual" only while measured `g` stays positive rather than
  promising it. (MSR-code proof-of-repair and the blind upgrade stay open, off the
  critical path.)
- **D-TAKEDOWN — ▶ DIRECTION DECIDED (low urgency); metric CONSTRUCTED.** Signed
  subscribable label layer + CT-style append-only transparency log + narrow opt-in
  denylist (memo 04); immutable #5 amended. The **formal non-globality metric** now
  has a construction (commission `A2-*`): a *survivor Nakamoto coefficient over
  failure domains*, published as a certified lower bound `≥ t` via a **ZK threshold
  predicate** that reveals only the scalar `t` (defeats the discovery-oracle). Only
  as real as the (non-cryptographic) independence oracle. → H9.
- **D-DEMAND — ▶ DIRECTION DECIDED (new).** Price standing on **cost-to-wash, never
  receipt count.** The blind demand receipt (Chaum/Compact-E-Cash token + PoR-bound
  delivery-ack + quorum-as-TTP optimistic fair exchange — **P2 abort-safety floor
  built** (`ExchangeCommitment` + token-not-consumed-on-abort + commitment-can't-redeem;
  the TTP dispute-RESOLUTION half is gated on the verifiable-escrow primitive —
  Camenisch–Shoup, no pure-Go impl; threshold-decryption/DKG itself IS available via
  kyber — an H7-strategy fast-follow, low-stakes under demand-neutrality)) delivers
  unforgeable-delivery + fetcher-unlinkability, but **demand *authenticity* is a
  Douceur limit** — self-dealing is uncloseable by any receipt. Re-price wash via
  burned fee + bonded-fetcher credential (commission `B2-*`) — **both levers now
  built + regression-locked** (P3a fee-burn; P3b `RequireBondedFetchers`: demand
  counts distinct bond-distinct fetchers, so wash costs one on-chain storage bond
  per faked unit). D3 issuance-mixing is a shared hard dependency for the
  unlinkability half. See `../decisions.md`.

## 10. Open problems — what could still sink this

> **Two companion references keep this section honest.** The full held-in-tension set
> — each residual classified (theorem / crypto-gap / scope / research-frontier), bounded,
> and given an open question for the research team — lives in
> [`owned-residuals.md`](owned-residuals.md). Its dual, every property M0 asserts as
> *delivered* mapped to the test that backs it, lives in
> [`claims-ledger.md`](claims-ledger.md) (CI fails if a ledgered claim's test disappears).
> A load-bearing property should be in exactly one of the two — never neither.

The thesis is a **design thesis, not a proof.** It argues that composition-in-
tension is the *only* route the impossibility results leave open, that it is
on-tenet (B8's licensed novelty), and that it converts the Sybil corner from an
impossible detection problem into a hard-but-approachable measurement problem. The
follow-up commission (`research-outcome/commission/`) sharpened this considerably:
**C1 (no discount) is now a theorem under three stated hypotheses**, and the
open residue narrows to *one* construction task plus the held-in-tension bounds §7
already names. What could still sink it:

- **⭐ THE shared-content sealing boundary (the single surviving economy of
  scale — the core open research task).** `B5-*` proves C1 as a **direct-product
  cost bound under H1 (per-axis linear hardness) + H2 (Leontief
  non-substitutability) + H3 (cross-identity independence)** — but H3 *fails on one
  axis*: if standing is ever granted for "I can pass a proof over shard *s*," then
  because erasure coding means many honest nodes legitimately hold the *same*
  shards, one physical copy answers for N pledges and the disk axis collapses
  **γ→1/N**. Closed only by upgrading the disk axis from proof-of-storage to
  **identity-keyed PoRep *sealing* of arbitrary useful shared data** — which is not
  yet publicly-verifiable + timing-free + trusted-setup-free. **Note this is
  *distinct from* A1 proof-of-repair** (A1 proves a repair was *correct*, a
  different axis; it does not make one copy stop answering for N pledges).
  **silt is not exposed *today*** — standing comes from a *dedicated* identity-keyed
  bond plot (`seed = H("silt/bond/plot/v3" ‖ pk ‖ n)`, so N bonds need N× distinct
  sealed disk), **not** from the shared shards a node serves; the disk axis and the
  served-demand axis are deliberately *separate* (safe, but the bonded disk is
  "wasted" throwaway labels rather than useful content). The open problem is
  **fusing served useful content into identity-keyed standing without leaking
  γ→1/N** — the single highest-leverage question, routed to an academic
  collaborator. *The claim "C1 holds" is gated on this staying separate (or being
  closed by sealing).*
- **MSR / regenerating-code proof-of-repair (open, off the critical path).** The A1
  proof-of-repair composition is airtight for *plain-RS* reconstruction (what silt
  ships); no published construction specializes it to MSR/Clay regenerating codes,
  where a helper sends a projected sub-symbol. Needed only if silt later adopts
  regenerating codes — a roadmap item, not an M0 blocker (commission `A1-*` G1).
- **Operator identification without a trusted authority (C2 — held, not closed,
  *by theorem*).** C2 must count *operators*, not keys; Kwon et al.'s impossibility
  makes this heuristic at its base — you cannot assign a Sybil cost to
  identity-splitting without a TTP. The achievable bound is `k* ≥ k̂ / M` (M =
  M_cluster·M_est·M_sample, the concentration-underestimate factor), so **shed only
  when k̂ ≥ k·M.** The **honest whale** (real infra-independent operator who
  *chooses* to collude) is outside C2 entirely — bounded only by the HHI/Gini veto
  + economic co-trigger + anchor wheels. C1 can hold while C2 fails; no cost bound
  will ever close C2 (`B1-*`, `B5-*` §4.5).
  - **A-axis pricing (built, weak by construction).** The shed now counts
    **address-diverse** participants, not just bond-distinct ones: each bond commits a
    declared failure-domain (`BondReg.Domain`, from `-domain`), and the maturity gate
    uses `min(NakamotoOperators, NakamotoDomains)`, so a stake split across many keys
    in ONE domain does not shed the wheels — a splitter must clear `k·M` distinct bonds
    **and** `k` distinct domains. This turns the flat margin M into a per-network-position
    cost. **Honestly weak:** the domain is *declared* (transport-cross-checked at H5-B,
    not proven) and network positions are rentable, so it **prices** concentration, it
    does not **close** it — the honest whale who buys distinct real /24s remains the
    owned residual. And the concentration itself is now surfaced out-of-band (HHI, Gini,
    top-share + a ⅓-capture alarm), measurement never enforcement (Kwon).
- **Byzantine size-estimation under *adversarial* NodeID placement (flagged gap).**
  The CPR estimator tolerates `O(n^{1−δ})` Byzantine nodes *when randomly placed*;
  a stake-splitter chooses its NodeIDs, degrading the bound by an amount **the
  literature does not quantify** — the least-supported load-bearing claim under C2
  (`B1-*`). A genuine research gap, not to be papered over.
- **Reachability of maturity (held in tension).** Conditional on the mature regime
  being reached before the young, anchor-scaffolded regime is captured. No proof;
  a **safe-parameterization** (plural threshold anchors + a **one-way maturity
  latch** — `B3-B4-*`), not a theorem. Immutable #3.
- **Anchor shed is one-way; de-maturation is defined, not silent (F-1 — resolved +
  owned).** The maturity shed is a genuine **one-way ratchet**: once the network is
  first certified mature, an `everMature` latch (a replay-derived *consensus* fact,
  not a live recompute) retires the launch anchors **permanently** — a later drop in
  decentralization, even an honest whale concentrating *real* bond, can never re-arm
  them. This closes the F-1 dual (a de-maturing chain no longer halts on absent
  anchors, nor hands a zero-bond anchor permanent power). What **replaces** the anchor
  net in the de-maturation window is a **real-bond super-quorum** (≥⅔ of live bonded
  weight, no anchor sign-off) — center-less liveness that preserves accountable
  safety. Two residuals are **owned, not closed:**
  1. **Bounded re-centralization.** After the latch, a genuinely re-concentrated
     *real* bonded set keeps committing under the super-quorum — caught only by C2 and
     the honest-whale residue above (the per-key margin `M`, the A-axis diversity cost,
     the HHI/Gini veto), never by re-arming anchors. This deliberately trades the
     (eliminated) permanent-center and undefined-halt risks for a **bounded,
     socially-recoverable re-centralization risk** — the same trade Ethereum, Cosmos,
     and Bitcoin made when they retired their own training wheels. Irreducible for any
     weakly-subjective system.
  2. **Weak-subjectivity dependency (newly explicit).** The latch makes silt an
     explicitly **weakly-subjective** system: a node syncing from genesis or long
     offline cannot distinguish the real matured chain from a forged long-range one on
     chain data alone, so it **must** be pinned to a recent trusted **weak-subjectivity
     checkpoint** (`-ws-checkpoint HEIGHT:HASH`, which refuses any reorg at or before
     it) obtained out-of-band, within the WS period (~`BondTTLBlocks` + slashing depth).
     This was *implicitly* true before — the old live-`Mature()` recompute never gave
     cold-boot safety — so the latch makes it **honest and mechanized**, not newly true.
- **Real-demand > wash-demand (held in tension).** C1's anti-wash is a **monitored
  economic inequality** `c_wash > c_real`, satisfiable with margin (independent
  reputation-weighted requester + protocol skim + served-bytes floor), not a proof
  — demand *authenticity* is a Douceur limit (`B2-*`, `B3-B4-*`).
- **Cold-data solvency (held in tension).** Perpetual solvency exists only for
  `g > 0`; ship finite-but-renewable and instrument `g` (D-S7, `A1-*` §4).
- **Parameterization (necessarily adaptive).** `C_honest`'s factors, the shed
  threshold *k* and its margin *M*, the audit window, the demand-attestation ratio
  are principled in *form* (φ=1/3, ordering invariant, one-way ratchet, k₀·M
  structure) but their *levels* must track live telemetry — never genesis
  constants. Where a young network is most fragile.
